Sticker Saver – Privacy Policy

Vesper Apps · Effective date: 30 September 2026

Sticker Saver ("the app"), made by Vesper Apps, lets you keep stickers you save in the TikTok app and add them to WhatsApp's sticker tray. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it. In short: your stickers, and everything the app sees in TikTok, stay on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google's ad software collects some device information to do that, as described under Advertising below.

What the app accesses, and why

AccessWhy it's used
Accessibility Service Only while the TikTok app is open, to notice when you tap a sticker and then tap Save, and, when you choose Import saved stickers and open TikTok's sticker panel yourself, to open its ★ Saved tab and scroll through it for you. The app only watches the TikTok app. It does not read or interact with any other app, and it does not record what you type.
Screen capture Used to copy the image of a sticker from the screen, including its animation. Android asks for your permission each time this is turned on. Only the small area of the screen showing the sticker is kept.
Display over other apps Only if you switch on the optional floating capture button, which lets you draw a box around a sticker on screen.
Photos you choose Only when you tap Make a sticker. Android's photo picker shows your photos; the app only ever receives the one photo you pick, and it's turned into a sticker on your phone. It is never uploaded.
Files you choose Only when you tap Add stickers from files, or share or open a file with the app. The app only reads the files you pick, and adds the stickers in them on your phone.
Internet Used only by Google AdMob to load ads. The app never uploads your stickers or anything it sees in TikTok.

Data stored on your phone

Stickers you capture are saved in the app's private storage on your phone, together with a small fingerprint of each one so the same sticker isn't saved twice. Once you add stickers to WhatsApp, the app deletes its own copies. WhatsApp sticker packs you create are kept by the app for as long as they are in WhatsApp, because WhatsApp reads them from the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps)
  • diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data

Google may use this information to show personalised ads where you have allowed it. In the European Economic Area, the UK and other places where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before this happens, and you can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (under Google › Ads, or Privacy › Ads, depending on your phone).

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Cutting out backgrounds

The sticker editor's Cut out button uses Google ML Kit, which runs on your phone through Google Play services. Your photo is analysed on the phone and is not sent to Google or to Vesper Apps. Google Play services may download the tool from Google the first time it's needed, and ML Kit sends Google information about how the tool performs, such as device model and timing, to keep it working well.

Purchases

The app offers one optional purchase, Remove ads, paid once. The payment is handled entirely by Google Play — Vesper Apps never sees your card or payment details. The app keeps a single yes/no setting on your phone recording whether ads have been removed, and asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the purchase so it can be restored on a new phone or after reinstalling.

Sharing

The app does not send your stickers, or anything the Accessibility Service sees, to Vesper Apps or to anyone else. The only data that leaves the app is the device and ad information collected by Google AdMob, described above. Stickers only leave the app when you choose to add them to WhatsApp, which happens directly on your phone through WhatsApp's own sticker pack feature. WhatsApp's use of those stickers is covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

No accounts or tracking by us

The app has no sign-in, and Vesper Apps does not run its own analytics or tracking. The third-party services in the app are Google AdMob (ads), Google Play Billing (the optional purchase) and Google ML Kit (the Cut out tool).

Deleting your data

You can delete stickers inside the app (hold a sticker, or use Settings › Delete all waiting stickers). Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. You can switch off the Accessibility Service at any time in your phone's Settings › Accessibility.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and Vesper Apps does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Other apps

Sticker Saver is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by TikTok or WhatsApp.
