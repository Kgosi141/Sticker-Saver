# Sticker Saver

Keep the stickers you save in TikTok and add them to WhatsApp's sticker tray — animated ones included.

- Tap **Save** on a sticker in TikTok's comments and Sticker Saver keeps a copy
- Import your TikTok **Saved** stickers — the newest few, or all of them
- Make your own stickers from photos, with automatic cut-out
- Add them to WhatsApp in packs of up to 30

Made by **Vesper Apps**. Sticker Saver isn't affiliated with TikTok or WhatsApp.

## Download

Get the latest version from the **[Releases page](../../releases/latest)** — download the `.apk` file under *Assets*.

Needs Android 11 or newer, plus TikTok (or TikTok Lite) and WhatsApp.

## Install

1. Open the downloaded `.apk` file.
2. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps**.
3. If Google Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap **More details › Install anyway**.

## Turn on sticker detection

Sticker Saver uses Android's accessibility service to see stickers in TikTok (only TikTok). Nothing it sees leaves your phone.

1. In Sticker Saver, tap **Import saved stickers** and follow the short guide.
2. In **Settings › Accessibility › Installed apps**, tap **Sticker Saver** and switch it on.

**Switch greyed out, or a "Restricted setting" message?** Android 13 and newer lock this switch for apps installed from a downloaded file. To unlock it:

1. Open **Settings › Apps › Sticker Saver** (the guide in the app has a button for this).
2. Tap **⋮** at the top right, then **Allow restricted settings**.
3. Go back and switch Sticker Saver on.

## Only download it from here

This page is the only official place to get Sticker Saver outside Google Play. Copies from anywhere else may have been changed — the app checks itself and refuses to run if it has been modified, so a changed copy won't work anyway.

To check a download yourself, compare it with the fingerprints listed on its release:

- **File SHA-256** — must match the checksum in the release notes.
- **Signing certificate SHA-256** — always `0f601f6aea9bbc8f33a4dd345a517bcc80309fa1e52c5097c46b5599c05e5902` (check with `apksigner verify --print-certs`).

## Updates

The app doesn't update itself. Check the [Releases page](../../releases/latest) for new versions — install a new `.apk` over the old one and your stickers stay.

## Privacy

Your stickers stay on your phone and only leave it when you add them to WhatsApp. The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Read the full [privacy policy](privacy-policy.html).
